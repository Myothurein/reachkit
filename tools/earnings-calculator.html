Why RPM Varies So Much Between Creators

  • Audience location beats view count: 100K views from US/UK/Japan viewers can easily out-earn 1M views from lower ad-spend regions.
  • Video length unlocks mid-rolls: On YouTube, videos over 8 minutes allow multiple ad breaks, often lifting RPM by 30%–60%.
  • TikTok CRP counts qualified views only: Replays, views under 5 seconds, and non-For You traffic do not earn payouts, which is why our calculator adjusts for your qualified view rate.
  • Don't rely on ad revenue alone: Pairing ad payouts with brand deals and affiliate links frequently multiplies creator income by 2x–5x.
