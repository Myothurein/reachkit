4 Script Pacing Rules for Higher Watch Time

  • Nail the 3-second hook (~8–10 words): Your opening sentence should deliver curiosity or value before viewers have time to swipe away.
  • Target 155–175 WPM for Shorts & TikTok: Vertical feeds reward tight pacing with minimal filler words and jump cuts between sentences.
  • Hit the 8-minute mark for YouTube mid-rolls: Writing around 1,100–1,250 words at a natural 145 WPM reliably clears 8 minutes with room for visual breathing space.
  • Use [bracketed cues] in your draft: Keep editing notes like [zoom in] or [show chart] inside square brackets so they never distort your true spoken runtime.
