Why Safe Zones Matter for Retention

  • Prevent blocked captions: Auto-captions placed too low get hidden behind the username, description, and music ticker.
  • Protect right-side callouts: Like, comment, bookmark, and share icons occupy the lower-right 13% of the screen.
  • Cross-post once, fit everywhere: If you design inside the TikTok bottom margin and Reels top margin, your video will stay clean across all three platforms.
