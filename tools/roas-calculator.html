Break-Even ROAS Quick Reference Table

The formula is simple: Break-Even ROAS = 1 ÷ Profit Margin (%). Higher margins let you scale ads profitably at much lower ROAS.

4 Media Buying Rules for Profitable Scaling

  • Never judge ads by ROAS alone: A 2.0x ROAS loses money on a 40% margin product, but prints cash on a 75% margin digital offer. Always benchmark against your Break-Even ROAS.
  • Watch your Max CPA inside Ads Manager: Set your Break-Even CPA as a hard stop-loss rule so underperforming ad sets get paused before draining your budget.
  • Raise AOV to lower Break-Even ROAS: Bundles, order bumps, and free-shipping thresholds increase profit per order without paying extra for clicks.
  • Track POAS (Profit on Ad Spend): POAS divides gross profit by ad spend. Anything above 1.0x means your campaign is net profitable after product and shipping costs.
