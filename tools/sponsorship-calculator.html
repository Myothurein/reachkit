4 Brand Deal Negotiation Rules That Protect Your Income

  • Price on average views, not followers: Brands buy guaranteed attention. Use the median view count of your last 10 organic uploads to justify your quote.
  • Never give away perpetual usage rights for free: If a brand wants to run your video as a paid Facebook or TikTok ad, charge 25%–50% extra per 30–90 days.
  • Lead with your Anchor Rate: Start 20%–25% above your target quote so you have room to compromise without dropping below your floor.
  • Pitch a 3-post bundle: Instead of discounting a single video, offer 15% off when the brand commits to a 3-video campaign.
