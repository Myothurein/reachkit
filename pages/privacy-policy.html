Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information is (and is not) collected when you use ReachKit.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Short Version: ReachKit tools run locally in your web browser. We do not require accounts, and we do not upload, view, or store your images, videos, scripts, or calculator inputs on any server.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

ReachKit does not require user registration or logins. All files selected in our Safe-Zone Previewer, scripts pasted into our Script Reading-Time Calculator, and financial figures entered into our Earnings, Sponsorship, or Ad ROAS calculators are processed strictly client-side in your browser memory.

2. Local Browser Storage & Preferences

We use your browser's localStorage solely to remember user interface preferences such as your selected Light or Dark theme. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

3. Analytics & Log Files

Like most websites, ReachKit may use privacy-respecting analytics services (such as Google Analytics or Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand overall traffic trends, popular tools, device types, and referring pages. These services may collect standard technical data such as anonymized IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps. We use this aggregate data solely to improve site speed and build better tools.

4. Cookies & Third-Party Advertising Partners

To keep ReachKit free, we may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdSense, Adsterra, Monetag, Ezoic, or Mediavine). These third-party ad vendors may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites across the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • You can also disable or clear cookies anytime through your individual web browser settings.

5. Affiliate Links

ReachKit participates in affiliate marketing programs with selected software and creator platforms (such as vidIQ, ElevenLabs, Shopify, or Payoneer). When you click a clearly marked partner or affiliate link, the partner website may place a tracking cookie in your browser to credit ReachKit if you make a qualifying purchase. Please review the respective privacy policies of any external websites you visit.

6. Information You Send Voluntarily

If you email us directly via our Contact page, we receive your email address and message contents solely for the purpose of replying to your inquiry. We never sell, rent, or trade your email address to third parties.

7. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area, UK, and California), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you have voluntarily shared with us via email, and to opt out of personalized ad cookies via your browser or ad-choice tools.

8. Children's Privacy

ReachKit is intended for general audiences, creators, and business professionals aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as we add new tools or advertising partners. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at hello@reachkit.tools or through our Contact page.
