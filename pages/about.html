Built to Save Creators & Marketers Time Every Day

ReachKit is an independent hub of free, fast, privacy-first web utilities designed for video creators, social media managers, and performance marketers.

Why We Built ReachKit

Running a modern content channel or paid ad account involves dozens of small, repetitive checks: making sure TikTok UI icons do not cover your captions, estimating realistic sponsorship rates before replying to a brand, timing a voiceover script, or checking break-even ROAS before scaling an ad set.

Most tools online hide these simple workflows behind account sign-ups, bloated spreadsheets, or slow paywalls. ReachKit puts the everyday utilities creators and digital marketers actually need into one clean, instant-loading workspace—completely free to use.

How Our Tools Work

Every calculator and previewer on ReachKit is engineered to run 100% locally inside your web browser using standard HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. When you upload a video frame into our Safe-Zone Previewer or paste a confidential script into our Script Reading-Time Calculator, your files and text stay on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

How ReachKit Stays Free

To keep every tool accessible without subscriptions or login walls, ReachKit is supported by non-intrusive display advertising and clearly labeled partner recommendations (affiliate links) for software we genuinely believe helps creators grow. Using our affiliate links costs you nothing extra and helps us maintain and build new utilities.

Zero Sign-Up Friction

Open any tool, enter your numbers or drop a file, and get answers in seconds—no email gates or accounts required.

Privacy by Architecture

All calculations and image/video previews happen client-side in your browser so your creative assets remain private.

Current Industry Benchmarks

Our safe-zone margins, RPM ranges, and sponsorship CPM tiers are maintained for 2026 platform specifications.
